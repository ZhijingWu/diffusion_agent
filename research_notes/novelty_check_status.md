# Novelty Check Status

A complete external novelty search was **not** performed as part of this package.

Therefore, avoid claims such as:
- “we are the first to discover this”;
- “no one has reported this issue”;
- “this finding is novel.”

Safe wording:

> We did not find this behavior discussed in the materials we examined, and our reproduction provides a direct source-level probe, quantitative audit, and controlled repair.

Before public posting or a formal write-up, search:
- the official repository Issues/PRs and commit history;
- arXiv/ACL/OpenReview follow-up discussions;
- subsequent papers citing GTD;
- independent reproduction notes.

The Proxy-insensitivity observation should especially be treated as supporting evidence rather than the primary novelty claim, because later work may already discuss related homogeneous-feature / graph-scorer limitations.
